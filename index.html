The Problem & Solution

Healthcare reimagined
through intelligent conversation

The Challenge

Doctors spend up to 40% of consultation time
just collecting history.

This inefficiency affects healthcare quality and patient satisfaction, creating a costly bottleneck in modern medical practice — limiting the time physicians have for what truly matters: diagnosis and treatment.

Our Solution

Veritas conducts the interview
before the doctor walks in.

An AI-powered clinical assistant that runs comprehensive pre-consultation interviews — gathering patient and family medical history through natural conversation. By appointment time, a complete, structured history awaits the physician.

Core Capabilities

Everything a clinical
intake needs — and more.

Intelligent Conversations

Natural language processing adapts to each patient's communication style, asking relevant follow-up questions based on symptoms described.

Structured Data Output

Automatically converts conversations into standardized, EHR-compatible formats that integrate seamlessly with existing hospital systems.

Multi-Modal Input

Supports both voice and text interactions, letting patients communicate in their preferred mode for maximum accessibility and comfort.

Clinical Summaries

Generates concise, actionable summaries highlighting critical information for rapid physician review before consultation begins.

Privacy First

Built with healthcare compliance at its core — full HIPAA adherence and encrypted data handling throughout the entire process.

Empathetic Design

Emotion recognition adjusts conversation tone and pacing, creating a supportive environment for patients discussing sensitive health issues.
